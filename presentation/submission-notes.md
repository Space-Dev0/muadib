# Presenter notes — 12-slide submission deck

1. **Introduction.** A diffusion model repeatedly denoises random noise under a condition. Our motivating question is whether familiarity with separate concepts is sufficient to combine them.
2. **Literature.** DDPM supplies the denoising foundation; latent diffusion scales conditional generation; compositional diffusion studies combinations of concepts; MOSAIC is adjacent research on compositional generation. Our study aims to measure constituent and joint exposure under controlled conditions. We do not claim the broad topic is new.
3. **Hypotheses.** H1 predicts a negative composition gap that becomes larger with relational complexity. H2 predicts that at fixed predicate marginals, the gap increases with joint lift, more so for relational rules. The separate complexity-versus-imbalance pilot does not establish either hypothesis. “Left of” compares horizontal coordinates and requires distinct objects, not a common row.
4. **Methodology and networking.** Render scenes with known metadata, measure frequencies of individual and joint predicates, construct training mixtures, train matched models, generate fixed samples, and review them blindly. Exposure-design validation is unfinished. A CPU producer sends PNG scenes and annotations over framed TCP. Run it over an authenticated SSH tunnel, because the prototype itself does not authenticate or encrypt peers. The localhost benchmark processed 300,000 scenes per batch setting; the cross-laptop SSH-tunnel trials used 300 each. They include rendering and encoding; neither measures GPU idle or physical link capacity. An opt-in trainer wrapper passed tiny same-host CPU parity and resume checks. Separate checkpoint-transfer code uses gzip, a byte offset for interrupted-transfer resume, and SHA-256 validation; hashing is not authentication. An image-level inference prototype distributes fixed color/seed jobs, checks checkpoint and image hashes, retries failed jobs on other workers, and records timing. Its existing loopback tests use a mock sampler; this does not yet demonstrate distributed GPU training or inference speedup.
5. **Architecture.** The forward formula corrupts a clean image using Gaussian noise according to the time-dependent schedule. A time- and condition-aware U-Net learns a velocity target. At generation the reverse process iterates from noise. The diagram is schematic.
6. **Dataset.** Synthetic shapes permit exact structural annotations. The four-cell pilot has 200 fixed images per cell. The 57-rule exposure matrix comprises 5.7 million accepted synthetic scenes; it is a separate instrument, not 57 categories evaluated in the pilot. A newer geometry-paired candidate supports held-out color–shape combinations.
7. **Setup.** Each of four matched PilotUNets trained for 500,000 updates, using color balance 50:50 or 90:10 and two or three objects. Generation was conditioned on red or blue; exactly two/three objects was a secondary human label, not the requested condition. Eight noise seeds are reused across conditions and are not independent training runs.
8. **Results.** Counts are yes/no/unresolved per cell from 16 images. The contrast bounds cover only assignments of uncertain labels in this finite set. They are not confidence intervals. Four returned review files are byte-identical; independent reviewer authorship is not demonstrated.
9. **Qualitative examples.** These are actual generated samples chosen to explain two different review outcomes, not randomly selected or representative results. Stochastic generation does not have a unique paired ground-truth target image.
10. **Ablation and failure cases.** The pilot changes input factors, not model components, so do not present it as an architecture ablation. Checker abstentions include every reviewed negative, leaving false acceptance unmeasured. The segmentation model reached 0.935 foreground IoU on 100 held-out blurred synthetic scenes. A separate balanced 500-scene checker dataset gave 63/100 decisions and 37 abstentions in its test split; all accepted decisions agreed with renderer metadata. These results are synthetic and do not validate generated-image checker performance. The rule adapter answered only 54 of the 300 previously reviewed generated images, compared with 95 for the baseline. Separate exposure-design diagnostics still fail.
11. **Discussion.** The directional comparison is descriptive, not evidence of generalization. Two training-seed blocks are insufficient for a reliable population comparison. The completed second block reuses fixed training data and cannot establish dataset-level replication; the team confirmed that its four reviews were independent, although the identical files do not independently verify identities.
12. **Conclusion.** The core contribution is the proposed controlled measurement framework and its validation work. Stage 0 has not passed. Validate checker errors, resolve design identifiability, and verify multi-device execution before causal claims.
