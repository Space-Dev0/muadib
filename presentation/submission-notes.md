# Presenter notes — 12-slide submission deck

1. **Introduction.** A diffusion model repeatedly denoises random noise under a condition. Our motivating question is whether familiarity with separate concepts is sufficient to combine them.
2. **Literature.** DDPM supplies the denoising foundation; latent diffusion scales conditional generation; compositional diffusion studies combinations of concepts; MOSAIC is adjacent research on compositional generation. Our study aims to measure constituent and joint exposure under controlled conditions. We do not claim the broad topic is new.
3. **Hypotheses.** H1 concerns joint exposure with similar individual exposure; H2 is a separate, smaller toy-pilot question. Neither is established by the current results. “Left of” compares horizontal coordinates and requires distinct objects, not a common row.
4. **Methodology.** Render scenes with known metadata, measure frequencies of individual and joint predicates, construct training mixtures, train matched models, generate fixed samples, and review them blindly. Exposure-design validation is unfinished.
5. **Architecture.** The forward formula corrupts a clean image using Gaussian noise according to the time-dependent schedule. A time- and condition-aware U-Net learns a velocity target. At generation the reverse process iterates from noise. The diagram is schematic.
6. **Dataset.** Synthetic shapes permit exact structural annotations. The four-cell pilot has 200 fixed images per cell. The 57-rule exposure study is a distinct future analysis, not 57 categories evaluated in the pilot.
7. **Setup.** Each of four matched PilotUNets trained for 500,000 updates, using color balance 50:50 or 90:10 and two or three objects. Generation was conditioned on red or blue; exactly two/three objects was a secondary human label, not the requested condition. Eight noise seeds are reused across conditions and are not independent training runs.
8. **Results.** Counts are yes/no/unresolved per cell from 16 images. The contrast bounds cover only assignments of uncertain labels in this finite set. They are not confidence intervals. Four returned review files are byte-identical; independent reviewer authorship is not demonstrated.
9. **Qualitative examples.** These are actual generated samples chosen to explain two different review outcomes, not randomly selected or representative results. Stochastic generation does not have a unique paired ground-truth target image.
10. **Ablation and failure cases.** The pilot changes input factors, not model components, so do not present it as an architecture ablation. Checker abstentions include every reviewed negative, leaving false acceptance unmeasured. Separate exposure-design diagnostics still fail.
11. **Discussion.** The directional comparison is descriptive, not evidence of generalization. One training-seed block is insufficient. The second block reuses fixed training data and cannot establish dataset-level replication.
12. **Conclusion.** The core contribution is the proposed controlled measurement framework and its validation work. Stage 0 has not passed. Complete the second block and review, validate checker errors, and resolve design identifiability before causal claims.
