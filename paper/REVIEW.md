# Independent critical review

A GPT-6.1 Sol (medium reasoning) agent reviewed the manuscript and repository evidence on 17 November 2026. Its verdict was **reject as a completed research paper**: the main joint-versus-marginal exposure intervention has not been run. The four-cell pilot and exposure-matrix analysis are feasibility work, not a test of the paper's central hypothesis. An ICML-style layout does not change that assessment.

The review identified additional major issues:

- Target circles are always red or blue and distractors have different colors and shapes. The evaluated target combinations occur in training; the pilot does not test novel attribute recombination.
- Model initialization and random streams were matched, but the scenes are not geometrically paired. Each cell has one dataset realization, so data-realization effects remain.
- Binding and existential event semantics require explicit treatment. Marginal event products do not generally describe a same-object conjunction.
- The outcome contrasts need definitions; their difference reduces to one pairwise cell difference. Eight noise seeds are not independent model-training runs.
- Four byte-identical files do not establish independent reviewers. Checker agreement on an accepted subset containing no negatives does not establish specificity.
- A frozen intervention, an identifiable estimand, model-seed and dataset replication, stronger checker validation, and publicly verifiable artifacts are needed before a strong scientific claim.

The revised paper clarifies these limitations, includes the contrast equations, separates constructed illustrations from measurements, and adds color-stratified and failure-case graphics. It does not claim to resolve the experimental deficiencies. The official ICML template is used for formatting; conference submission requires independent compliance, anonymity, artifact and research-readiness checks.
